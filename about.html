SP International is an India-based merchant trading company. We help importers, distributors and specialty buyers source turmeric powder, red chilli powder and cumin seeds.

We are a buyer–seller bridge, not a factory. We listen to the product requirements, check Indian suppliers against the brief, and come back with a written quotation.

From destination and packing to the documents your market needs, the details are discussed in the sourcing conversation. More spices and brass / art-metal décor are available on request.
